# MacroSnap support

Contact us from the app (Settings > Support), or use the email address on the [home page](./). We reply within two business days.

## Common questions

**How do I cancel Pro?**
Open the Settings app on your iPhone, tap your name, then Subscriptions, then MacroSnap. Cancel at least 24 hours before the renewal date.

**How do I restore a purchase?**
In MacroSnap, open Settings > Subscription > Restore purchases.

**How do I export my data?**
Settings > Data > Export. You get a JSON file with everything in your account.

**How do I delete my account?**
Settings > Delete account. This removes your account, your data and your uploaded photos from our servers and your phone. It cannot be undone.

**Why won't it let me pick an earlier goal date?**
Targets never go below a safe floor. If a date needs a faster pace than that, MacroSnap shows the earliest safe date instead.

**Is this medical advice?**
No. Talk to a doctor before starting a diet if you are pregnant, have a medical condition, or have a history of disordered eating.
